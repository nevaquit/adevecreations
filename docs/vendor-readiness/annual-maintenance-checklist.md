# Annual Maintenance Checklist

## Registration and Profile Maintenance

- [ ] Review NCAGE registration status and renewal requirements
- [ ] Review SAM entity registration record for accuracy
- [ ] Confirm UEI remains active and properly mapped to legal entity
- [ ] Validate company profile content (services, contacts, address)

## Compliance Refresh

- [ ] Re-issue NDAA telecommunications declaration
- [ ] Re-validate local tax compliance statement
- [ ] Confirm VAT handling statement remains accurate

## Evidence and References

- [ ] Validate at least three active references with current contacts
- [ ] Update capability statement PDF with latest date
- [ ] Archive previous versions and changelog entries

## Cadence

- Monthly: monitor procurement opportunities and update opportunity log
- Quarterly: refresh references and capability evidence
- Annually: full registration and compliance review
